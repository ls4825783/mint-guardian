How a mint earns a “safe” verdict

The three gates behind a standard safe verdict. A mint that fails any gate is listed as risky or skipped — never quietly promoted. The single historical exception, THE WALL, is named as an override in its own report rather than hidden.

1

Contract verified

The mint contract must be verified on Sourcify (exact match) with readable source. Unverified contracts are an automatic reject, however good the audit looks.

2

Audit risk LOW

The Binance / security audit must come back LOW with no drainer patterns, hidden mint authority, or non-standard token behaviour. MID or worse does not pass.

3

Clean value-0 preview

The mint transaction is simulated first: price must be 0 on-chain, no token approvals, no authority changes. Only then is it minted — and the receipt becomes the report’s evidence.
